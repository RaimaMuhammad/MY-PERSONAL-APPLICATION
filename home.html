About Muhammad Raima

One Voice, One Vision, A Lasting Impact

Muhammad Raima is a Ugandan youth leader, international debater, public speaker, climate and sustainability advocate, and emerging technologist. She currently works in youth leadership and debating while pursuing Information Technology and Computing Systems at Uganda Christian University. Her work focuses on youth empowerment, technology, sustainable development, public speaking, and creating opportunities for young people to participate, lead, and turn ideas into meaningful action.

Her journey has taken her from student leadership and competitive debating to representing Uganda at the World Schools Debating Championship in the Netherlands, Vietnam, Serbia, and Macau. She has also participated in international and regional platforms, including the International Olympic Academy in Greece and the Africa Regional Forum on Sustainable Development. Alongside debate and advocacy, she continues to explore technology as a tool for solving real-world challenges while contributing to initiatives focused on climate action, education, and community development.
