ABOUT MUHAMMAD RAIMA

Muhammad Raima is a Ugandan youth leader, international debater, public speaker, climate and sustainability advocate, and emerging technologist. She is pursuing Information Technology and Computing Systems at Uganda Christian University. Her work focuses on youth empowerment, technology, sustainable development, public speaking, and creating opportunities for young people to lead and take action.

Her journey has taken her from student leadership and competitive debating to representing Uganda at the World Schools Debating Championship in the Netherlands, Vietnam, Serbia, and Macau. She has also participated in international platforms such as the International Olympic Academy in Greece and the Africa Regional Forum on Sustainable Development. She continues to explore technology as a tool for solving real-world challenges.


HER JOURNEY

Muhammad Raima's journey began with student leadership, public speaking, and a passion for creating positive change. Through debating, she discovered the power of a voice, the importance of listening to different perspectives, and the responsibility that comes with leadership. Through this she wanted to create opportunities, contributr to her community and solve real world challenges.

Her journey soon moved beyond the classroom. She represented Uganda on international debating platforms and participated in youth leadership and development programmes. These experiences gave her opportunities to engage with young people, global issues, education, climate action, and sustainable development.
